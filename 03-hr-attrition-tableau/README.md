# HR Attrition Dashboards (Tableau)

Academic work from *Advanced Data Analytics for Business* (CIA II, Feb-Mar 2025).

## Data
Course-provided HR dataset: 1,470 employee records (the Excel file is included). The brief named Tableau and Power BI; **only Tableau was used here.**

## What was built
Two dashboards: satisfaction by department, attrition rate by age band, employee count by marital status and job role, distance from home, current vs ex-employees by department, training by qualification, average salary by job role, and years at company vs attrition.

## Figures on the dashboards (checked against the Excel data)
- Attrition by age band: under 25 = 39.18%, 25-34 = 20.22%, 35-44 = 10.10%, 45-54 = 10.20%, over 55 = 15.94%
- 237 ex-employees: R&D 133, Sales 92, HR 12
- Average years at company: 7.37 (stayed) vs 5.13 (left)

## Files
`HR_Data.xlsx`, `HR_Dashboard_1.png`, `HR_Dashboard_2.png`, assignment brief screenshot.

## Known limitations
- Some chart titles do not match the metric: "Gender Diversity Ratio" shows marital status, and "Average Training Hour" plots training times last year.
- The performance pie shows summed ratings, which is not a meaningful measure.
- No written insights are included.
